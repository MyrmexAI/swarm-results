Shared: none of the three is a 1:1 peg; the token is a share of backing SOL, so the SOL price should rise as rewards accrue. Selling on a DEX can trade off the on-chain rate. Solana slashing “does not really exist right now,” per Jito’s FAQ. Validator commissions still come out before these protocol fees.

DEX TVL and quoted APY were not available: Jupiter, Marinade, and DefiLlama hosts returned HTTP 403 from this environment on 11 October 2026. Do not treat the single-epoch growth rates above as APYs.
